01About

A software engineer with a business lens.

I’m a Computer Science and Business student at Northeastern’s Khoury College of Computer Sciences and a Software Engineer Co-op at NExT Consulting, where I build dashboard interfaces and AI-assisted analysis for RepoPulse — a platform that helps professors and TAs keep a pulse on student GitHub projects.

Most of what I build lives where software meets operations: an instructor dashboard for student repositories, a multichannel order tracker for e-commerce sellers, and a trading assistant with a risk engine between every idea and the broker. Across all of them I follow the same rule — let AI handle judgment calls like summarizing, classifying, and drafting, and keep the math and the guardrails in deterministic, tested code.

The business half of my degree keeps me focused on the people using the software — the instructor grading a group project, the founder juggling four marketplaces, the client who just needs their website to work.

An automated trading platform that monitors a live stock watchlist, evaluates market triggers, and uses Claude to generate structured BUY, HOLD, SELL, WATCH, or AVOID recommendations — with a risk engine standing between every idea and the broker.

  • Designed a risk engine that checks maximum open positions, stop-loss rules, and confidence before any trade executes.
  • Connected to Alpaca Paper Trading for automated order execution, broker-side protective stop-losses, and real-time position tracking.
  • Implemented dynamic position management with partial profit-taking, confidence tracking, and automated sell conditions.
  • Dashboard logs every recommendation, rejection, and order for a complete audit trail.
