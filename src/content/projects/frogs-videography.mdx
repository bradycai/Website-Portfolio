## The job

Frogs Videography makes mini documentaries, event recaps, commercials, and fundraising videos for businesses and nonprofits in Massachusetts and Rhode Island. I designed and built a new site to replace their Squarespace one, moved it to Netlify, and stayed on for technical support.

## What's on the site

The homepage walks through the company's services, a portfolio of client videos, the production process, the team, and an ROI calculator that estimates what a video project could open up for a business or nonprofit.

## Built to be easy to update

- Nearly all of the copy lives in one config file, grouped by section, so text changes rarely touch the components.
- Every section can be switched off with a single flag, without deleting code.
- A portfolio entry only needs a YouTube video ID. The thumbnail, hover state, lazy-loaded player, and alt text are generated from it.
- The contact form degrades gracefully until an email service is connected.
- Animations respect the reduced-motion setting.

## Launch

The site is a static Astro build. I moved hosting to Netlify, set up the custom domain, DNS records, redirects, and HTTPS, and handled account access, domain settings, and integrations through the internship.
